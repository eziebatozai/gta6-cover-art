# Scene plate credits

The eight photographs in this folder are the app's built-in "scene plates". The two starter
images (`boulevard.jpg`, the woman by the car, and `pier.jpg`, the angler) were made with an
image model for make.fun and are free to use. The six Wikimedia Commons photographs are public
domain or CC0 as listed; files were fetched as 1280px thumbnails and kept as-is.
